    Frequently asked questions

    Is this a medical diagnosis?

    No. It is a statistical estimate based on patterns in a dataset. It cannot replace a clinician.

    Is my data stored?

    This interface does not store anything. Inputs are sent to the prediction API and the result is shown in your browser.

    How accurate is the score?

    Accuracy depends on the training data, which covers a specific student population. Treat the score as a guide, not a measurement.

    What does the score range mean?

    90 and above is excellent, 70 to 90 is healthy, 50 to 70 is moderate, and below 50 needs attention.
